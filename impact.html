Partnership Impact

Working together to transform schools and communities.

Since 2021, the Ntodeni Nenungwi Foundation has partnered with schools, educators, community leaders and the Vhembe East Department of Education to create opportunities for young people across the district.

Our impact is built through mentorship, learner recognition, leadership development, anti-bullying campaigns, conferences and school-based support.
