About Ntodeni Nenungwi Foundation

Empowering, inspiring and developing young people in rural South Africa.

Who We Are

Creating safe spaces where learners can learn, grow and thrive.

The Ntodeni Nenungwi Foundation was established in 2021 with a vision to empower, inspire and develop young people in rural South Africa.

At the heart of our work is the belief that every child deserves access to mentorship, positive role models, educational opportunities and life-changing experiences that prepare them for a successful future.

Our Vision

To create a society where every young person has access to mentorship, guidance, education and opportunities.

Our Mission

To empower young people through mentorship, leadership development, educational support and community engagement.

Our Reach

NNF continues to expand across Limpopo, working with schools and community partners.

Our Founder

Driven by mentorship, education and perseverance.

Since its inception, NNF has worked closely with the Vhembe East Department of Education to deliver impactful school-based programmes focused on leadership development, confidence building, academic excellence, career guidance and personal growth.

From mentorship programmes and educational support initiatives to leadership conferences and learner recognition awards, our programmes address social and educational challenges while helping young people realise their full potential.
