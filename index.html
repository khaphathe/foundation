Ntodeni Nenungwi Foundation

Empowering Young People. Transforming Communities.

NNF is a South African non-profit organisation committed to empowering young people through mentorship, education, leadership development and community engagement.

Learn More Partner With Us

Founded in 2021

Established to address challenges facing young people in rural communities, particularly the boy child.

About NNF

Empowering today’s youth for a brighter tomorrow.

Through strategic partnerships, school-based programmes, conferences, awards and educational initiatives, we equip learners with confidence, skills and support to succeed academically, socially and personally.

For over five years, NNF has created opportunities for young people to discover their potential, embrace positive values and become responsible leaders within their communities.
